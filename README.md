### 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm **Tasfia Zaman**, an Adjunct Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at [BRAC University](https://www.bracu.ac.bd/).

My research interests lie in **AI safety, mechanistic interpretability, and machine unlearning**. I'm particularly interested in understanding how the internal representations of language models relate to their observable behavior, especially when the two appear to diverge.
